Why the apparent winner failed scrutiny

The first agent analysis favored coffee after selectively excluding February. The challenge revealed that coffee’s record February 2024 value also drove the change. In ten leave-one-month-out tests, the growth leader changed five times. The original 2025 growth gap was only about 0.15 percentage points.

Untrusted claim: “Energy drinks are the faster-growing category.” The tiny apparent lead is unstable. February 2025 energy interest (49.3) is unusual but not proven wrong and was retained in the primary analysis.

Data trust and limitations

94 monthly observations (2018–October 2025); no months deleted. The agent reported 11 primary seasonal flags and 24 review-only flags under the revised two-test screen. Two restaurant review files were excluded from category evidence because neither mentions coffee or energy drinks; one is explicitly SYNTHETIC. Search interest is national and cannot establish regional demand, sales, or profit.

Decision standard

Confidence: Low. Approve a controlled pilot, not a broad reallocation. Continue only if measured incremental profit supports expansion.
